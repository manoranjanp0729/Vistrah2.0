# VISTRAH contextual photography

Real, locally bundled stock photographs. They illustrate subjects and do not depict VISTRAH staff, listed providers, available properties, advertised employers or completed VISTRAH work. No AI-generated assets.

New selections were verified on official Pexels free-photo pages on 9 October 2026. Existing source/licence records are retained. Exact downloads, verification evidence, crop notes and hashes are in `frontend/lib/photo-assets.json`. Both recorded licences allow web use and modification without mandatory attribution; credits are retained here.

The registry and explicit reuse rationale live in `frontend/lib/image-registry.json` and `image-reuse.json`; see `docs/imagery/REPORT.md` and the generated inventory for missing specialist subjects.
