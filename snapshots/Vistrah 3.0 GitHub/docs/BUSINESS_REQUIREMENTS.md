# VISTRAH 3.0 — business requirements

Status: **Confirmed** unless explicitly marked **Pending** or **Proposed**.

## Audience and permissions

- Customers browse/search services, contact sellers via messaging or calls, request services and quotations without buying a seller membership.
- Sellers/businesses upgrade their existing account, select a monthly plan and undergo administrator approval. They publish approved services, receive enquiries, advertise and recruit.
- VISTRAH administrators approve sellers/listings, configure plans and VISTRAH-owned services, and oversee enquiries, marketing and hiring. Seller-managed services are priced by sellers subject to approval; VISTRAH-owned services by administrators. Customer service prices are independent of seller memberships. Pricing modes: fixed, starting from, quotation-based.

## Monthly seller memberships

| Benefit | Basic | Plus | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | ₹149/month | ₹799/month | ₹1,899/month |
| Listing/search eligibility and organic enquiries | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Social creatives per billing month | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| Social campaigns per billing month | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| Campaign duration | — | up to 7 days | up to 7 days each |
| AI video shorts | 0 | 0 | 1/month, up to 30 seconds |
| Included social advertising spend credit | ₹0 | ₹200/month | ₹500/month |
| Extra social advertising spend | separately billed by seller choice | separately billed by seller choice | separately billed by seller choice |
| Mobility billboard | separately quoted | separately quoted | priority booking assistance and separately quoted |
| Reporting | listing and leads | basic campaign report | detailed campaign report |
| New job posting credits per successful renewal | 5 | 15 | 50 |

Premium allowances are totals, not Plus benefits added twice. Social ad credits apply toward advertising spend, not creative production/management. Any additional exposure requires seller choice and payment. No guaranteed leads, reach, conversions or billboard availability.

## Job credits

Unused job credits carry forward; each issued batch expires **90 days after issuance**. Successful monthly renewal adds a new batch without extending earlier batches. Use earliest-expiring credits first (proposed implementation rule). Job seekers can browse/apply without seller membership.
